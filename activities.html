Our Activities

Discover ways to get involved in our community garden.

Community Gardening

Join us to grow fresh vegetables, herbs, and flowers in our shared garden beds. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced gardener, there's always something new to learn and enjoy. Spend time outdoors, share gardening tips, and watch our garden grow together.

Gardening Workshops

Discover practical gardening skills through friendly, hands-on workshops. Learn how to sow seeds, care for seedlings, make compost, and grow food in small spaces. Our workshops are a chance to ask questions and share ideas with other garden enthusiasts.

Volunteer Days

Help keep Green Corner growing! Join our community volunteers to water plants, maintain garden beds, and prepare for the changing seasons. It's a relaxed way to meet neighbours, enjoy the outdoors, and make a positive difference in our community.
