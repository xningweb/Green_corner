About Our Garden

Green Corner is a small community garden where people come together to grow plants, share knowledge, and enjoy nature.

Our Story

Green Corner was created to bring a little more greenery into our neighbourhood. We believe that gardening is a wonderful way to connect people and build a stronger community.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a welcoming space where everyone can learn about gardening, grow fresh food, and care for the environment.
